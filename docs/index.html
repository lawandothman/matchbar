What it does

67′

Lives in the menu bar

The score sits beside your clock, minute ticking, only while matches are on. The rest of the time it's just a quiet ⚽.

⚽️

Goal notifications

Scorer, minute and score, about thirty seconds behind the broadcast. Own goals (og), penalties (pen), and disallowed goals get a notification too.

Twelve group tables

P W D L GD Pts, dense as the back page of a newspaper. Knockout bracket when we get there.

Every fixture, your timezone

The week ahead, sectioned by day. Extra time, penalty shootouts and simultaneous kickoffs all handled.

Honest small print

  1. Free, and stays free.
  2. No analytics, no accounts, nothing phones home except the scores.
  3. Native Swift. Uses about as much memory as this web page.

Scores via ESPN's public scoreboard feed, polled every 30 seconds during matches.
